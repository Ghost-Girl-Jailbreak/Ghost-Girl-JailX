> 👻 **Ghost Girl JailX** bundles and repackages software created by the PS3 homebrew community. **None of the tools below were made by us** — full credit belongs to their original developers. Huge thanks to every one of them for keeping the PS3 alive. 💜

<br/>

<div align="center">

### ⚡ <span style="color:#FFD700;">Evilnat</span>
**Custom Firmware (CFW)**

The CFW base that powers Ghost Girl JailX on compatible consoles.

👤 **Developer:** [Evilnat](https://github.com/Evilnat)
🔗 **Repo:** [github.com/Evilnat/Evilnat-CFW](https://github.com/Evilnat/Evilnat-CFW)
📜 **License:** GNU GPL v3

</div>

---

<div align="center">

### 🌐 <span style="color:#FFD700;">webMAN MOD</span>
**Web Server, Fan Control & Game Launcher**

Lightweight web-based manager for launching games, controlling fan speed, and managing your PS3 remotely.

👤 **Developer:** [aldostools](https://github.com/aldostools)
🔗 **Repo:** [github.com/aldostools/webMAN-MOD](https://github.com/aldostools/webMAN-MOD)
📜 **License:** GNU GPL v3

</div>

---

<div align="center">

### 💽 <span style="color:#FFD700;">multiMAN</span>
**Backup Manager & File Browser**

One of the most iconic PS3 backup managers — rips, launches, and organizes your game backups.

👤 **Developer:** [DeanK](https://www.psx-place.com/) (Dean Kasabow)
🔗 **Thread:** [psx-place.com multiMAN thread](https://www.psx-place.com/threads/multiman.20970/)
📜 **License:** Freeware / Community

</div>

---

<div align="center">

### 🛰️ <span style="color:#FFD700;">Apollo</span>
**Save Data & Trophy Management**

Powerful save editor and trophy manager for PS3 — backup, restore, and transfer saves with ease.

👤 **Developer:** [bucanero](https://github.com/bucanero)
🔗 **Repo:** [github.com/bucanero/apollo-ps3](https://github.com/bucanero/apollo-ps3)
📜 **License:** GNU GPL v3

</div>

---

<div align="center">

### 🗂️ <span style="color:#FFD700;">IRISMAN</span>
**Backup Manager & NTFS Support**

Feature-rich backup manager with NTFS read/write support, FTP server, and prepNTFS tools.

👤 **Developer:** [aldostools](https://github.com/aldostools) (based on original IRISMAN by Alexander)
🔗 **Repo:** [github.com/aldostools/IRISMAN](https://github.com/aldostools/IRISMAN)
📜 **License:** GNU GPL v3

</div>

---

<div align="center">

### 🎯 <span style="color:#FFD700;">Artemis</span>
**Real-Time Cheat Engine**

The successor to PS3 Cheat Engine (PS3CE) — apply real-time cheats to games via `.ncl` files.

👤 **Developer:** [bucanero](https://github.com/bucanero) (based on original by **davidps3**)
🔗 **Repo:** [github.com/bucanero/artemis-ps3](https://github.com/bucanero/artemis-ps3)
📜 **License:** GNU GPL v3

</div>
